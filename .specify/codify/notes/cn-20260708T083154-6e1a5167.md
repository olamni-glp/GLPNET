Bounded-behavior spec promises (e.g. 'very large schema documents get bounded, deterministic behavior') must be enforced in the implementing task itself: a hard cap constant + a loud named error + an adversarial regression test, all landing together. In 043, the promise existed but counted-repeat NFA expansion and three recursive walks shipped unbounded; the gap was only caught by a 6-cycle post-hoc review (25 defects). Rule: no bounded-behavior promise without its cap+error+test triple in the same task.
