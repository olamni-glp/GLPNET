A blind 3-builder run with pairwise-disjoint slices, a cross-provider codex critic and a mechanical merge CORROBORATED A FALSE FINDING THREE WAYS, because every slice was fed from the same collection method (an SMB export that hides the working volume). Disjointness protects against a bad READER; it does nothing against a bad COLLECTOR - it corroborates the artefact and dresses it in a corroboration count. The independence audit passed because it inspects RECORDED INPUTS, not the COLLECTION PATH. Four peer lanes found the identical exposure in their own runs once it was published. REMEDY: every 3rtask manifest slice must declare its collection method, and a run whose slices share one method must be flagged before the merge is trusted.
