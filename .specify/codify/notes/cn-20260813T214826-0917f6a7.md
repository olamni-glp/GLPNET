The distributed allocation design's central structural finding, worth encoding as a first-class scheduler concept: three per-host lanes run in parallel but are NOT independent at two points. SHIP TOKEN - at most one host in ship->close fleet-wide, because buildkit ship destroys trust material other lanes pin. SYNC TOKEN - at most one host in a roadmap sync round, because the sync round is the 13th unit of EVERY per-feature chain and is a fleet-shared resource; its completion receipt must be a PEER-SIDE IMPORT, never a local replay-verify PASS (both hosts once reported PASS while holding divergent catalogs 19/107/2856 vs 18/105/3023). INTEGRATE INTO TOOLING: give bk-scheduler a fleet-exclusive-resource primitive (a named token a WP must hold to enter a stage) so ship and sync serialise mechanically instead of by convention.
