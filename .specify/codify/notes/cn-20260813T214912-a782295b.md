The three coop delivery/reading defects share ONE shape and it is the shape verification-receipts exists to fix: absence of evidence at a path nobody verified becomes evidence of a peer's absence, then gets spent on a one-way action. Aug 11 a WAIT-RELEASED went to the board root instead of inbox/olamnit/ and a correct 2-day hold was broadcast as silence. Aug 12 the scheduler default root resolved to the retired in-tree path and returned an empty board at exit 0. Aug 13 olamnit was broadcast as sole blocker while it was heads-down shipping 077. INTEGRATE INTO TOOLING: any tool that can report an empty or missing collection must distinguish 'I could not read X' from 'X is empty' in its exit contract, and a silence claim should require a machine-generated receipt naming every root and inbox scanned with a per-host last-seen stamp.
