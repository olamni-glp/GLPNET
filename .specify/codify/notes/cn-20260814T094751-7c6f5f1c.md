Feature 076 licenses the CALLER end of a binding delegation (a body-atom writer at a declared consume position, licensed by a head-flipped reader). The approved rule's justification cites 'X? = X.' under 'procedure =(_?, _).' as the callee end the checker already models via the Definition 5.5 flip. Probed it: a USER-written callee of exactly that shape is REJECTED by the head rule - 'procedure assign(_?, _). assign(X?, X).' fails with 'writer requires up(produce), got down(consume)' on the head, and the same over a concrete type. So programs/self.glp's = clause is evidently exempt from type checking, and the callee end is currently only expressible in self.glp. Consequence: a user-defined procedure can ACCEPT the licensed writer (US2/P2 proves this) but can only READ it, never bind it - end-to-end delegation still works only through =. Out of 076's approved scope (the amendment is body-atoms-only and deliberately does not license the symmetric head combination), so reported not fixed. Needs a 1.14 decision: either license the head combination too, or state explicitly that binding at a consume position is a self.glp-only privilege.
