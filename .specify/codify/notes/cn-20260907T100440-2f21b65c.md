MEASURED SHIRAS 2026-09-07T10:03Z. ynetd.py board reports root=/home/shira/.local/share/ynet/root - a PER-USER, PER-HOST board, not the shared /mnt/gavri/d/coop/ynet. This is the exact failure resolve_board_root() documents from ARIELLAS 2026-09-05 ('the trio came up healthy on all three ports and federated with NOBODY, because every lane was reading a private board no peer could see'), which engineer ruling Q-ARI-YNETROOT-01 was supposed to retire. It is still live on SHIRAS. WRITES ARE FINE - broadcasts 000015/16/17 all reached the shared board and the highest record id is identical on both (000075), so nothing outbound was lost. THE READ REPLICA IS SHORT BY EXACTLY ONE RECORD: term-6 records number 11 on shared and 10 in the replica, and the missing one is 'broker@gavris prepare for broker@ariellas 09:44:26Z'. Prepares for broker@ariellas are therefore 6 on shared (= quorum) and 5 in the replica (< quorum). That single dropped record is the entire difference between 'leader elected' and 'fleet leaderless': the daemon's logic is correct, its input is one marginal record short, so it correctly finds no quorum, correctly falls back to term 5, and correctly reports leader:None. guardian@gavris's prepare at the SAME timestamp IS present, so replication half-committed a simultaneous pair - the defect is batch atomicity, not lag. WHY IT MATTERS BEYOND ONE ELECTION: if each host folds a slightly different subset of ballots, hosts will disagree about who leads forever, and every host will be certain and correct on its own evidence. That is the F-1 generator, and it plausibly explains every 'fleet is leaderless' P0 raised today including one already retracted. DURABLE FIX: make the daemon read the shared root directly (Q-ARI-YNETROOT-01 already rules it correct) - this removes the class rather than the instance - or make the replica write atomic per batch. DIAGNOSTIC EVERY LANE CAN RUN: 'ynetd.py board' and read the root field; if it is under the home directory, that lane's elect output is unreliable. NOT FIXED BY RESTART ON PURPOSE: term 6 sits at exactly 6 of quorum 6 and SHIRAS holds a backing pair, so bouncing broker+guardian to clear a READ bug would risk the quorum the read bug is misreporting.
