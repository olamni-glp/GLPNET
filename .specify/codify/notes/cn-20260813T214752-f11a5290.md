buildkit-scheduler emits a FABRICATED schedule when no durations are seeded: 'critical_path (6 WPs, 0.0h)' with P50 = P80 = P95 = the current hour. Three identical percentiles on a zero-duration path is not a thin estimate, it is an invented one, and a reader who quotes plan output without inspecting its inputs publishes a number with no evidence behind it. INTEGRATE INTO TOOLING: when every WP on the path has zero/absent duration, plan and cycle must REFUSE to emit P50/P80/P95 and finish, and print an explicit 'no duration evidence - structural view only' banner, emitting the dependency DAG and chain length in stage-units instead. CPM without durations is not a critical path.
